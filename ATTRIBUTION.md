## Exercise illustrations

`public/exercises/` holds the 302 exercises × 3 frames of
[Workout Guide](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide) by [Bryl Lim](https://bryllim.com),
based on pose artwork from [Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data) (created by Greg Priday).

They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The upstream `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `LICENSE-ASSETS.txt` are kept beside them, and
`sources.json` records the creator, licence and upstream source of every frame.

The files are used unchanged. In the app they are recoloured with a CSS filter only.
Changes to the images themselves must be shared under the same licence.

## Third-party code
